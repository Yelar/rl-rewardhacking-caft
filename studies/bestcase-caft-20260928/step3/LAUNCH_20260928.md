Stages, in order:
0. Specs for the off and pair conditions, then smoke tests of the Mrh + LoRA + CAFT engine (4 problems, 64 tokens,
   projection and structural zero).
A. Probe redo: 10,000 answers from the step-200 adapter, then sandboxed grading.
B. Energy calibration on Mrh, then energy-matched random controls for the 6 pair candidates.
C. Validation generation for 13 conditions (off, 6 pair candidates, 6 random controls): 149 problems × 10 samples each.
D. Probe fit on the step-200 data, then 4 probe conditions (chosen block, blocks 22–27, and a random control for each).
E. Grading of all 17 conditions (sandbox), then the summary with the pre-declared selection.

Declared changes from protocol draft v1, section 6:
- Every candidate has its own energy-matched random control (6 + 2) instead of one per block set. This gives exact
  energy matching for each candidate.
- The energy calibration uses Mrh, the model step 3 intervenes on, instead of base. Base is still used for step 4.
- Each problem uses the same seed in every condition (common random numbers).
- Eligibility requires both standard and stripped accuracy to meet the 5-point limit, as in guardrail G2.
